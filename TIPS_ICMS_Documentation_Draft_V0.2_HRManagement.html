7. Staff Exit Process

Comprehensive exit management from resignation to final clearance.

7.1 Employee Relieving Counselling Form ▼

Initial form for staff resignation with exit interview details.

Counselling Purpose

  • Retention Attempt - Understand reasons, address grievances
  • Knowledge Transfer - Document responsibilities, handover planning
  • Feedback Collection - Exit interview, improvement suggestions
  • Relationship Management - Maintain positive relations, alumni network

Grid: Campus, Staff Name, IdNumber, Staff Type, Programme, Designation, Department

7.2 Counselling Form List ▼

List of staff who underwent exit counselling with status tracking.

Status Options

  • Scheduled - Session planned
  • Completed - Form filled
  • MD Approved - Managing Director approved
  • Pending - Awaiting completion

Grid: Campus, Staff Name, IdNumber, Type, Programme, Designation, Department, Status, Rpt/Coor Name, PDF

Departments Involved

Call Management Module

Comprehensive issue tracking and resolution system for student-related issues and support requests.

Workflow States

✅ Available ▼
  • New issues entered into the system
  • Awaiting assignment to staff members
  • Visible to administrators and team leads
🎯 Assigned ▼
  • Issue assigned to specific staff member
  • Staff member notified
  • SLA timer begins

🟡 Below 24 hrs
🟠 24-48 hrs
🔴 SLA Breached
